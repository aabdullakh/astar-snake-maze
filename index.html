A* Snake Maze

A snake hunts a goal through a maze. A* finds the cheapest route across the grid, diagonals included, then the route is straightened and rounded so the snake glides like a real one. Watch the search decide which cell to try next.

Click or drag on the maze to add or remove walls. Drag A or B to move them.

How A* chooses

f = g + h

g
cost of the steps taken from A to this cell. A straight step costs 1 and a diagonal step costs 1.41.
h
guess of the cost left to B, ignoring walls: Manhattan distance with 4 directions, octile distance with 8.
f
g plus h, the predicted cost of a full route through this cell.

Each tick of the search:

  1. Take the open cell with the lowest f. Open cells are amber.
  2. Mark it closed. Closed cells are blue.
  3. Look at its neighbors. If a neighbor is floor and this route reaches it more cheaply, record the new g and remember this cell as its parent. A diagonal step is only allowed when both cells beside it are free.
  4. Stop when the cell taken is B, then follow the parents back to A.

After the goal is found:

  1. With "Smooth the path" on, any waypoint with a clear straight line past it is dropped. The dashed line is A*'s grid path and the solid line is the smoothed route.
  2. Corners are rounded, but only as far as the snake still clears the walls.
  3. The snake glides along the route at a steady speed, and its body follows the trail its head leaves.

In explore mode the maze is hidden by fog and the snake sees only a few cells around it:

  1. It runs A* on its own map, where unseen cells count as open, so the plan heads straight for B.
  2. It walks the plan a short way, then looks around and reveals what it can see.
  3. If a newly seen wall blocks the plan, it runs A* again from where it stands.
  4. It keeps its map, so later goals are easier. Compare "Travelled" with the shortest path to see what not knowing cost.

Things to try:

  • Switch Snake to "Explores the maze" and press Run.
  • Change Vision and watch the replans and the distance travelled change.
  • Switch Movement to 4 directions and compare the route.
  • Switch to Dijkstra and compare "Cells expanded".
  • Switch to Weighted A* and compare "Grid path cost" with "Cheapest grid path".
  • Turn off "Smooth the path" and watch the snake follow the grid.
  • Turn on "Show g, h and f in cells", then use Step.

The snake's body is only decoration until you turn on "Snake body blocks the path".
